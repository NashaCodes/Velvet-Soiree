Established 2024 • About Velvet Soirée

Food. Presentation. Experience.

Velvet Soirée is a catering and event planning brand based in Pretoria and Cape Town, created to bring together exceptional food, elegant presentation, thoughtful planning and memorable experiences.

We provide flexible catering solutions for private gatherings, corporate functions, celebrations, day outings and larger occasions. Each service is shaped around the event, guest count, preferences and overall experience our client wants to create.

“We believe catering should be more than a meal — it should be part of the experience.”
